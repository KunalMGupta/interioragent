- **Pattern:** One service wall — washer + dryer + folding counter + utility tub as ONE rigid
  GridGroup row on the back wall; shelf and airer face each other across the aisle from the two
  wall-CENTRE slots (so they share one grid row); ironing board on the front wall
- **Jitter/randomness:** RoomGroup randomness=0.1

  sink/shelf/rack/board across four rows auto-sizes a hall (see examples/laundry_room.md); also
  exposure (a window + the 500 W default blows a small white room out — light_budget=180)
